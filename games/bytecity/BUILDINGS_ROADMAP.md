# OmarchyCity: Buildings & Growth Stages Checklist

This roadmap tracks the design, illustration, and implementation of all building stages in OmarchyCity with a Studio Ghibli / Japanese aesthetic.

---

## 🏡 Residential Zones (3×3 Footprint)

Residential plots evolve from vacant staked lots, to clusters of single-family Japanese homes, to unified multi-family apartments and high-density estates. Multiple architectural styles are used per stage to avoid monotony.

### Stage 0: Surveyed Lot
- [ ] **Stage 0: Vacant Zoned Lot** (Tiles 240–248, Center 244)
  - Stakes, survey ropes, gravel border, grass clearing.

### Stage 1: Dispersed Japanese Homes (Pop 1–8, Tiles 249–260)
*Individual houses sprout around the 3×3 perimeter before merging into unified buildings. We provide multiple distinct Japanese architectural styles:*
- [ ] **House Style A — Traditional Minka / Suburban Showa Home**
  - Dark wooden timber walls, grey/blue ceramic tile roof (*kawara*), sliding lattice doors, small bamboo fence, clothesline.
- [ ] **House Style B — Taisho-era Western-Japanese Hybrid (*Bunka Jutaku*)**
  - Plaster walls, terracotta orange or matcha green steep hipped roof, bay window, flower planter boxes, bicycle parked outside.
- [ ] **House Style C — Traditional Machiya Townhouse**
  - Narrow wooden facade, vertical wood slats (*koushi*), clay roof tiles, small stone lantern, potted morning glories (*asagao*).
- [ ] **House Style D — Country Hillside Cottage**
  - Natural cedar siding, stone foundation, vegetable patch (cabbages/pumpkins), persimmon tree with orange fruit.

### Stage 2: Small Multi-Family Apartments (Pop 16, Den 0, 3×3)
- [ ] **Style A (Low Value / Rustic): Showa Wooden Boarding House (*Mokuchin Apart*)**
  - 2-story weathered dark wood exterior, external metal stairwell, corrugated tin roof, air conditioner outdoor units, meter boxes.
- [ ] **Style B (Standard / Charming): Post-War Danchi / Green Shingle Flats**
  - 2-story stucco apartment with moss-green tiled roof, laundry balconies, potted bonsai and shrubs along the front path.
- [ ] **Style C (High Value / Traditional): Compound Estate of Joined Annexes**
  - Multi-building wooden family compound, central courtyard with a black pine and stone stepping stones (*tobi-ishi*).
- [ ] **Style D (Luxury / Scenic): Taisho Villa with Veranda**
  - Polished dark cypress timber, wrap-around veranda (*engawa*), ornamental roof ridgelines, lantern courtyard.

### Stage 3: Mid-Rise Garden Apartments (Pop 24, Den 1, 3×3)
- [ ] **Style A (Modest): 3-Story Concrete & Brick Danchi**
  - Clean retro mid-century anime look, tile facades, drying futons on iron railings, bicycle shed at ground level.
- [ ] **Style B (Charming / Ghibli Coastal): Brick & Stucco European-Japanese Flats**
  - Red brick lower floor, warm yellow stucco upper floors, arched windows, chimneys, flower window boxes (*Kiki* style).
- [ ] **Style C (Traditional High-End): Ryokan-Inspired Residential Mansion**
  - Multi-tier curved Japanese gables (*chidori hafu*), wooden lattice screens, bamboo garden border.
- [ ] **Style D (Luxury Modern): Terrace Garden Apartments**
  - Step-back stepped terraces with flowering wisteria trellises, rooftop greenhouse or solarium.

### Stage 4: High-Density Urban Flats (Pop 32, Den 2, 3×3)
- [ ] **Style A (Dense Urban): 5-Story Retro Commercial-Residential Block**
  - Ground-floor noodle/tea shop with awnings, 4 stories of residential balconies above, water tank on roof.
- [ ] **Style B (Ghibli Taisho Roman): Classical Stone & Brick Residence**
  - Ornate brick lintels, decorative copper green cornices, arched entryway with gas lantern lamps.
- [ ] **Style C (Steampunk / Mechanical): Brass-Accented Urban Manor**
  - Intricate fire escapes, brass rooftop ventilation cowls, round porthole windows, slate roof tiles.
- [ ] **Style D (Luxury Penthouse Complex): Grand Courtyard Mansions**
  - Gated entrance, manicured pine courtyard, rooftop sun deck with potted trees.

### Stage 5: High-Rise Condominium Towers (Pop 40, Den 3, 3×3)
- [ ] **Style A (Classic Metropolis): Art-Deco Japanese Skyscraper Residence**
  - Streamlined limestone and teal copper spires, stepped crowns, warm glowing windows.
- [ ] **Style B (Ghibli Fantasy Tower): Spired Castle-Like Residence**
  - High copper roofs, clock tower or weather vane, hanging gardens and climbing ivy.
- [ ] **Style C (Modern Tokyo Vintage): High-Rise Danchi Complex**
  - Geometric retro balconies, rooftop water towers, aerial antenna arrays, community courtyard.
- [ ] **Style D (Imperial / Luxury Pinnacle): Pagoda-Inspired Sky Manor**
  - Modern luxury tower crowned with Japanese tiered eaves, bronze ornamental crests, rooftop glass pavilion.
